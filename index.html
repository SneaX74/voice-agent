Marson's Voice Assistant

A personal assistant you talk to. It answers out loud in Estonian or English, keeps replies short, and helps with business, studies and planning.

Try it in Gemini View on GitHub

What it does

Speaks your languageReplies in Estonian or English, whichever you use.
Built for voiceOne to three sentences, no lists or symbols, numbers said naturally.
Business and study helpIdeas for Sisulaine Media, EBS coursework, planning the day.
Knows the CVInterview practice, a 30-second intro, help with applications.
Asks before actingReads back messages and actions and waits for a clear "yes".
HonestSays when it doesn't know, and gives real opinions.

How to use it

  1. Have the Gemini app on your phone, or use gemini.google.com in a browser.
  2. Tap Try it in Gemini above, or open the shared Gem from this link. You need to be signed in to a Google account.
  3. Tap the microphone and talk. For example:
"Tere! Mida ma täna kõige esimesena tegema peaksin?"
"Give me three video ideas for a gym client."
"Ask me interview questions for a marketing job."
